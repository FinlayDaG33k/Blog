## Review Policy

If you want me to review your product, then that's great!  
However, I do not take payments, referral deals, revenue sharing agreements etc. etc.  
Additionally, I will *not* be a figure head for your product.   
You are free to send extra goodies (like merch or candy or something) but this will not affect the outcome of the review.  
All words in a review will be mine with the exception of potential correspondence.  
You as the supplier will only see the review when it comes online, just like everyone else.

### Review Sample Policy

If you wish to send me a product for review, please note the following:

- **No guarrantees**: There is no guarrantee any posts or mentions of your product will be made on any of my outreach. I'll do my best wherever I can but if I am simply see no value then I'm simply not gonna bother.
- **Destruction**: I often like to see the insides of things, so if it's not meant to be opened, things may get destructive.
- **One-way trip**: Review samples will be kept by me or be put up in a giveaway. In rare circumstances (eg. prototypes) can we arrange a return but the costs of this will be for you *and* this will only happen if previously agreed upon.
- **The gamble**: A review will be made of the product "as-is", so if it's broken or has a malfunction then that will make it into the review. You may get contacted about it, you may not. If it's a prototype of pre-production unit, then let me know before hand so I can take this into account. <img src="emotes/mjsriichi.png" width="16px">
