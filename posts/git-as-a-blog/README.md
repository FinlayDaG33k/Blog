# Git as a Blog

Some of those that caught up to this, may know that I used to make blogposts on my website, so why the sudden change, and why GitHub of all places?  

First, we'll need to go back years ago, to around 2018.  
Back in that day, I still worked as a developer and at the company I worked for at the time, I was tasked to make a new CMS so we could move off WordPress.
As I was building that CMS, I needed a place to test it as well, because I'd rather shoot myself in the foot than a customer.  
That test place, became my own website which was developed under the "Horizon" codename.
After years of working in development and working on the CMS, I was eventually laid off, forked the CMS and continued development on my fork.  
I did still spend year or so still developing it but eventually a lot of things happened and I no longer had the energy, time and interest in continuing development.
As such, the CMS was abandoned but still running behind my website.  
The same is also why I mostly stopped making videos on YouTube and streaming on Twitch in recent years.

As more time passes, things started breaking more and more, despite me not changing anything.  
So I would need to spend even more energy and time into fixing it all, which I didn't want.  
Around 2023 was when I started with the idea that I should maybe just migrate my blog elsewhere...  
The problem being that a lot of it was "custom written", so porting it would pose a major challenge...  
For which I once again didn't have the energy and time.  
Slowly, in the background, however, a second timebomb was ticking without me realizing.  
I had been using Imgur as a place to store images that were then also used on my website.  
However, what I didn't realize was that Imgur also deleted images silently.  
And as a result of this a lot of older images used on my blog were deleted without me knowing.  
Lost to time forever, demotivating me even more.  

However, in the background, people kept telling me I should keep writing blog posts and make videos because they find them educational and helpful at times.  
I did try making a video again, however, editing took a lot more time and energy than I was willing to put in, so it was sent to the archives instead.  
Though, due to people often running into the same issue over and over in a Discord server, I eventually updated the script a bit and instead turned it into a [blog post](https://github.com/FinlayDaG33k/Blog/blob/1ba885de2cba9cbf766c602c230815e39ef0d427/posts/wifi-authentication-methods/README.md).  

But now the question... Where do I put it?...
I had considered some places but they all had issues...  

* Go back to my subreddit? Doesn't write that well...
* Blogger? Do people still use that?
* Wix? I will run out of space in notime... And US$17/month is pretty steep for a silly blog. Also what if they do me dirty? I'll be spending a lot of time migrating *again*...
* WordPress.com? Yea no... I don't wanna fix my blog every week...

My criteria weren't all that big but the combination made it rather hard:

* Allow me to keep (most) of my workflow (which is to say, draft once, iterate many times, publish).
* Allow me to keep a history of iterations.
* Be cheap (preferably free).
* No vendor lock-in.
* Plenty support for platforms.
* Easy migrations if shit hits the fan.
* Little to no maintenance effort from my side.

Gee, I wonder what tool that I already used fit that bill...  
So I started experimenting with Git where we are now.
I hope that with this, I can finally focus more on writing, rather than everything around it.  
We'll see.

Thanks for reading!
