# Huion Violating EU Law

Due to having severe space limitations on my "desk" and my old G502's left-click having broken (it double-clicks unpredictably), I swapped over to using my old `Wacom Intuos S`.  
Because I do not like that the pen inputs a "click and hold" when I hold the pen against the tablet, I have set my left-click to the bottom button.  
While this works quite well, it has one big annoyance: The pen only has 2 buttons.  
As a result, I can only have left-click and scroll added to the pen.  
I currently have my right-click (which I also need quite often) bound to one of the express keys on the tablet itself.  
It is, however, very much not ideal to have to move my left hand all the way over to right-click.  
But it was that or having to do it when scrolling.  
As such, I started looking at a new pen-tablet, preferably one that has either 3 buttons or a scroll wheel.

After some looking around, I found the `Huion Inspiroy 2 S` and costing only 50 euro on sale on their European-storefront (this is a key detail for later), it was quite a good deal.

So on the 1st of November 2026, I placed my order and two days later, it was in my hands. <img src="emotes/kannayay.png" width="16px">

## It's... Not for me...

After I got it in, I hooked it up to my PC and gave it a test.  
Just a few minutes in and the annoyances already piled up:

- Lift-off is way too high and not configurable in software.  
  I prefer it to only start tracking once I touch the tablet itself (give or take a millimeter) so I have tactile feedback (this is also why I disabled my Wacom's default behaviour).
- Pen clicks when touching the tablet, which I've already mentioned is a huge deal for me.
- Pen heavily "twitches" when pressing buttons on pen (Probably just a driver issue? I was on the latest tho).
- Actual useable surface *way* smaller than the tablet itself (unlike my Wacom where your margins are maybe 1cm).
  This often left me with the mouse no longer moving without any form of tactile feedback.

As such, I wanted to return the tablet... Which became a rather huge hassle. <img src="emotes/haram.png" width="16px">
